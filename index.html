Questions

Good to know

What are Credits?

Credits are GateMC's store currency. Spend them in game on super powers with /powers and on crate keys with /crates. Credits never expire.

I bought something but didn't get it

Purchases usually arrive within a minute. If you were offline, join the server and they'll be delivered. Still missing after a few minutes? Ask staff in game and have your Tebex transaction ID ready (it's in your receipt email).

Can I buy for a friend?

Yes. Log in with your friend's Minecraft username before checking out and everything goes to them.

How do rank subscriptions work?

Ranks renew every month. You get the rank straight away and keep it for as long as the subscription runs. Cancel any time from Manage subscription; the rank stays until the end of the month you paid for.

What's the difference between a rank and a bundle?

Ranks on the Ranks page are monthly subscriptions. The rank inside a bundle is permanent and is paid once, and the bundle adds Credits on top.

I play on Bedrock. Does it work?

Yes. Join on gatemc.org port 19132, and log in here with your Bedrock name including the dot in front (for example .Steve).

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are final once delivered, and breaking the server rules never gets you a refund. If something never arrived, ask staff in game first. Chargebacks result in a permanent ban. Full details in our Refund Policy.

GateMC refund policy

  • Ranks, Credits and Bundles are digital items delivered to your account straight away, so all purchases are final once delivered.
  • If your purchase never arrived, ask staff in game with your Tebex transaction ID (it's in your receipt email) and we'll get it delivered.
  • Cancelling a rank subscription stops future payments. It doesn't refund the month you've already paid for, and you keep the rank until that month ends.
  • Credits that have been spent on super powers, crate keys or anything else can't be returned or refunded.

Breaking the server rules

Breaking GateMC's rules never gets you your money back.

  • If you're muted, banned or otherwise punished for breaking the rules, you won't be refunded for anything you've bought: ranks, rank time left on a subscription, bundles, Credits, or anything bought with Credits.
  • A ban doesn't cancel your rank subscription. If you don't want to keep paying, cancel it yourself (see Manage subscription).
  • Rules and punishments are at GateMC staff's discretion and can change at any time. A successful appeal restores your account, not a refund.

Chargebacks

Opening a chargeback or payment dispute instead of talking to us results in a permanent ban from GateMC. Ask staff in game first; most problems can be fixed quickly.

Ranks

Manage your rank

Rank subscriptions are billed and managed by Tebex. You can cancel any time; you keep your rank until the end of the month you've already paid for, and no further payments are taken.

Cancel or view your subscription

  1. Open Tebex's payment history. Go to checkout.tebex.io/payment-history.
  2. Enter the email you used at checkout. Tebex emails you a secure link. Open it on the same device.
  3. Find your GateMC rank. Your active subscriptions are listed with their next payment date.
  4. Click Cancel. No more payments will be taken. Your rank stays until the paid month runs out.
Open Tebex payment history ↗

Other ways to cancel

  • From your receipt: every Tebex email about your subscription has a cancel link.
  • Paid with PayPal: you can also cancel it in PayPal under Settings, Payments, Manage automatic payments. Look for Tebex.

Switching ranks

Tebex doesn't swap a running subscription to a different rank. To move to another rank, cancel your current subscription using the steps above, then buy the new rank from the Ranks page. If you skip the cancel, both subscriptions will keep billing.

Need help?

Can't find your subscription or don't have the email any more? Ask staff in game with your Minecraft username and roughly when you bought it.
